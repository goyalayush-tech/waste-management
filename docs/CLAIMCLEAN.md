# ClaimClean (Phase 1)

## What is ClaimClean
ClaimClean is a SaaS-plus-audit platform that verifies plastic waste recycling claims made by recyclers and submitted by brands (e.g., HUL, Nestlé, P&G) under India’s EPR rules.

## Gap We Address
- Brands pay recyclers/brokers to “recycle” X tons; invoices/photos/certificates are often fake or duplicated.
- Brands risk CPCB penalties, reputational damage, and double counting.
- No neutral, tech-driven verification layer exists.

## How ClaimClean Works
- Brands upload recycling claims (invoices, weighbridge slips, photos, GPS data, licenses).
- ClaimClean AI audits documents for duplication, metadata anomalies.
- Random physical audits by field partners (small %).
- Generates a Verification Report + ClaimClean Score.
- Brands can submit this report to CPCB/state boards as proof of due diligence.

## Revenue
- Subscription SaaS fees (₹25K–₹2L/month depending on waste volume).

## Why Now
- India’s PWM Rules 2016 + 2022 (EPR mandatory).
- CPCB fines & notices (2023–24). Brands want to de-risk; regulators want integrity.

## Market Size
- India FMCG + packaging EPR market = $2.5B+ annually.
- 5% verification share → $100–125M ARR opportunity.

## Real-world Penalties (user-provided examples)
- Enviro Recyclean Pvt Ltd: ₹175 cr fine (capacity mismatch).
- Shakti Plastics Industries: ₹128 cr penalty (excess certificates).
- Technova Recycling India Pvt Ltd: ₹46 cr fine (fraudulent certificates).
- Bisleri: ₹10.75 cr; HCCB: ₹50.66 cr; PepsiCo India: ₹8.7 cr for EPR non-compliance.

## Outcome
These penalties show fake/unverifiable EPR claims are a real risk. ClaimClean reduces fraud, protects brands from penalties, and builds regulator trust.
