# ClaimClean — PRD (Phase 1)

## The Problem
India's FMCG giants (HUL, Nestlé, Pepsi, P&G) must prove plastic recycling under EPR. Recycler claims are often fake, duplicated, or unverifiable, leading to massive penalties.

## Gap in the Market
No independent, neutral tech-driven verification layer. Brands risk fines and reputational loss. Regulators (CPCB/SPCB) demand proof but lack tools.

## Solution
- Audit recycler claims (invoices, photos, GPS, licenses).
- Detect duplication, tampering, anomalies.
- Random on-ground audits via field partners.
- Generate a Verification Report + Score for CPCB/state boards.

## Why Now
EPR mandatory (2022), aggressive enforcement since 2023; brands need neutral firewall against recycler fraud and penalties.

## Market
India plastic EPR ≈ $2.5B annually. First-mover advantage for neutral verification.

## Business Model
SaaS subscription; long-term expansion to SE Asia EPR regimes.

## Outcome
Brands save penalties and protect reputation; regulators get credible, tamper-proof evidence; ClaimClean becomes trusted compliance infra.
