Transparent fraud detection:

Justified, not hyped:
- On-Chain: Certificate hash, ID, timestamp, auditor signature, IPFS links
- Off-Chain: Images, OCR text, personal data (privacy + efficiency)

- Duplicate claims

- Cross-recycler laundering
