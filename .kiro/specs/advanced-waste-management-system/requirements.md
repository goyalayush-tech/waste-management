# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This document outlines the requirements for enhancing the existing Delhi Waste Management System into an advanced all-in-one platform that leverages cutting-edge AI/ML, blockchain, and Web3 technologies. Building upon the current system's foundation of waste tracking, AI classification, and basic blockchain integration, this enhancement will add unique features including decentralized governance, tokenized incentives, NFT-based waste certificates, and advanced predictive analytics to create a truly revolutionary waste management ecosystem.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Enhanced AI-Powered Waste Classification with Multi-Modal Analysis

**User Story:** As a waste management operator, I want the system to provide advanced multi-modal waste analysis combining visual, chemical, and weight data, so that I can achieve unprecedented accuracy in waste sorting and contamination detection.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN waste items are analyzed through multiple sensors (camera, spectrometer, weight) THEN the system SHALL achieve 98%+ classification accuracy through sensor fusion
2. WHEN contamination is detected in recyclable streams THEN the system SHALL automatically flag contaminated batches and suggest remediation
3. WHEN rare or valuable materials are identified THEN the system SHALL create special handling protocols and notify relevant stakeholders
4. WHEN waste composition changes are detected THEN the system SHALL automatically update processing parameters and alert operators

### Requirement 2: NFT-Based Waste Certificates and Digital Twins

**User Story:** As a waste producer or recycler, I want to receive NFT certificates for my waste contributions and access digital twins of waste processing, so that I can prove my environmental impact and track waste transformation in real-time.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN waste batches are processed THEN the system SHALL mint unique NFT certificates containing waste origin, processing method, and environmental impact data
2. WHEN waste items enter the system THEN digital twins SHALL be created to simulate and track their complete lifecycle transformation
3. WHEN NFT certificates are queried THEN they SHALL provide immutable proof of recycling achievements and carbon offset calculations
4. WHEN waste processing milestones are reached THEN the system SHALL automatically update NFT metadata with real-time processing data

### Requirement 3: Advanced Web3 DeFi Integration and Carbon Credit Marketplace

**User Story:** As a participant in the waste ecosystem, I want to trade tokenized environmental credits and participate in DeFi protocols, so that I can monetize my environmental contributions and access financial services.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN recycling activities generate carbon credits THEN the system SHALL tokenize them as tradeable assets on decentralized exchanges
2. WHEN users stake their environmental tokens THEN they SHALL earn yield through DeFi protocols and liquidity mining
3. WHEN carbon credits are traded THEN the system SHALL maintain transparent pricing and automatically distribute royalties to original waste contributors
4. WHEN environmental impact thresholds are met THEN the system SHALL unlock premium DeFi features and governance rights

### Requirement 4: AI-Powered Autonomous Waste Processing and Smart Contracts

**User Story:** As a waste facility operator, I want autonomous AI systems to control waste processing equipment and execute smart contracts based on processing outcomes, so that I can achieve fully automated waste-to-value conversion.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN waste batches are identified THEN AI systems SHALL automatically configure processing equipment parameters and execute optimal processing sequences
2. WHEN processing is completed THEN smart contracts SHALL automatically calculate and distribute payments to waste contributors based on material value recovered
3. WHEN quality thresholds are met THEN the system SHALL automatically trigger downstream processes and update supply chain partners
4. WHEN processing anomalies are detected THEN AI SHALL implement corrective actions and update machine learning models in real-time

### Requirement 5: Quantum-Enhanced Predictive Analytics and Digital Twin Ecosystem

**User Story:** As a city planner, I want quantum-enhanced predictive models and comprehensive digital twins of the entire waste ecosystem, so that I can optimize city-wide waste flows and predict environmental impacts years in advance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN quantum algorithms are applied THEN the system SHALL solve complex multi-variable optimization problems for city-wide waste flow in real-time
2. WHEN digital twins are updated THEN they SHALL simulate waste ecosystem changes and predict impacts on air quality, water systems, and urban health
3. WHEN long-term predictions are generated THEN the system SHALL provide 5-10 year forecasts with 95%+ accuracy for waste generation and processing capacity needs
4. WHEN scenario modeling is requested THEN digital twins SHALL simulate policy changes and infrastructure investments to predict outcomes

### Requirement 6: Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO) for Waste Ecosystem Governance

**User Story:** As a stakeholder in the waste ecosystem, I want to participate in a fully decentralized autonomous organization that governs waste management policies and resource allocation, so that I can have democratic control over environmental decisions and benefit sharing.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN governance proposals are submitted THEN the DAO SHALL use quadratic voting to prevent whale dominance and ensure fair representation
2. WHEN proposals pass THEN smart contracts SHALL automatically execute changes to system parameters, fund allocation, and policy implementation
3. WHEN treasury funds are managed THEN the DAO SHALL use multi-signature wallets and time-locked contracts for security
4. WHEN disputes arise THEN the system SHALL implement decentralized arbitration through randomly selected token holder juries

### Requirement 7: Metaverse Integration and Virtual Waste Management

**User Story:** As a digital citizen, I want to participate in waste management activities within virtual worlds and metaverse platforms, so that I can learn about environmental impact and earn rewards through gamified experiences.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN users enter the metaverse waste facility THEN they SHALL be able to virtually sort waste, operate equipment, and learn about recycling processes
2. WHEN virtual waste management tasks are completed THEN users SHALL earn real-world tokens and NFT achievements that transfer to the physical system
3. WHEN educational content is accessed THEN the metaverse SHALL provide immersive 3D visualizations of waste processing and environmental impact
4. WHEN virtual events are hosted THEN the system SHALL enable community gatherings, competitions, and collaborative waste management challenges

### Requirement 8: Cross-Chain Interoperability and Multi-Blockchain Integration

**User Story:** As a global waste management participant, I want to interact with the system across multiple blockchain networks and transfer my environmental assets seamlessly, so that I can participate in the global circular economy regardless of my preferred blockchain.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN users operate on different blockchains THEN the system SHALL enable seamless asset transfers through cross-chain bridges and atomic swaps
2. WHEN environmental tokens are moved THEN the system SHALL maintain consistent value and metadata across all supported blockchain networks
3. WHEN global waste data is aggregated THEN the system SHALL provide unified analytics across all blockchain networks and geographic regions
4. WHEN interoperability protocols are updated THEN the system SHALL automatically adapt to new blockchain standards and maintain backward compatibility
